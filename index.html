Implications for personalized recommendations

  • Build each user's baseline from repeated measurements rather than population averages.
  • Combine HRV with soreness, perceived recovery and performance after strength training.
  • For endurance, combine training load, sleep, HRV, nutrition and wellbeing.
  • For timing, record session end-time, intensity/strain and bedtime proximity.
  • Display uncertainty, data completeness and confounders; avoid diagnosis and causal claims.
